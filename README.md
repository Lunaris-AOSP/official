
# Lunaris AOSP Maintainers & Team Members Requirements

---

## 1. Eligibility

- To become an official Lunaris OS maintainer, you must:
- Have experience maintaining at max 2 other ROMs and atleast 8 months, with Lunaris OS being your third.
- Have at least 10 months of ROM build experience or a GitHub account that is 1 year old
- Be comfortable with building from source, resolving compilation errors, and troubleshooting device-specific issues.
- Have a solid understanding of Git, Android build system, and device tree structure.
- Proper contribution in device tree and follow a clean commit structure.

---

## 2. Source Cleanliness

Maintainers must ensure:
- At least two of the following repositories must be open-source and publicly available: device tree, vendor tree, and kernel source.
- Git history is clean, with no unnecessary merges, spam commits, or unrelated changes.

---

## 3. Commit Standards

- Follow proper commit naming conventions
- Use meaningful commit messages, avoid vague ones like “update” or “fix”.
- Always include your real name or recognized handle in the commit author field.
- Rebase instead of merging when possible to maintain a clean Git history

---

## 4. Device Maintenance Standards
- Ensure your builds boot, and maintain core stability.
- Test major functions such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Camera, GPS, Fingerprint, and RIL before release.
- Push regular updates
- Avoid introducing hacks or workarounds unless absolutely necessary, and document them when used.

---

## 5. No Unauthorized Actions
- Do not push updates without proper review and approval.
- Do not distribute, rebrand, or monetize rom without consent.

---

## 6. Support Expectations
- Respond to user-reported critical bugs within a reasonable timeframe.
- If you cannot continue maintaining a device, notify the team so a replacement maintainer can be assigned.
- Be respectful when interacting with users and other maintainers.

---

## 7. Inactivity Policy
- Maintainers who miss 2 consecutive monthly patches or remain inactive for more than 60 days (2 months) without prior notice will be automatically removed from the official maintainer list.
- No prior warning will be issued unless the maintainer has informed the core team of their absence in advance.
- Removed maintainers may reapply once they are able to actively maintain their device again.

---

## Collaboration & Communication
- Actively participate in official discussions and group chats.
- Report major issues, fixes, or improvements to the core team.
- Help new maintainers and contribute back to the ecosystem whenever possible.

---

**Thank you for your dedication to Lunaris OS!**
